# Flooding closes Vellmoor's main bridge; three people injured

*This is a fictional article written to test it's news. The town, the people
and the figures are invented.*

The River Vell burst its banks in the town of Vellmoor on Sunday night after
two days of heavy rain, flooding about 200 homes and closing the Mill Street
bridge, the only road crossing in the town centre.

Three people were taken to hospital with minor injuries, the fire service
said. About 450 residents spent the night in two shelters, at the town hall
and at Vellmoor High School.

The river peaked at 4.2 metres on Monday morning, above the previous record
of 3.9 metres set in 2014. The water had fallen to 3.1 metres by Monday
evening.

"The bridge stays closed until engineers have checked every pillar," said
Ana Brandt, the mayor of Vellmoor. "We would rather lose a week than take a
risk with people's lives."

Engineers will inspect the bridge on Wednesday. The council said repairs, if
needed, could take until the end of the month. Until then, buses will run a
detour of about 25 minutes through the Hollin Road crossing.

Tomas Reyes, who runs a bakery beside the bridge, said the water reached
"halfway up the counter" and that he hoped to reopen next week.

The council has opened a fund for families whose homes were damaged.
Applications open on Thursday.
