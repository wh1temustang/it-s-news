# Night train to link Vienna and Barcelona from April

*This is a fictional article written to test it's news. The operator, the
people and the figures are invented.*

A sleeper train will run between Vienna and Barcelona three nights a week from
April 9, the operator Nocturne Rail said on Thursday, the first direct
overnight link between the two cities since 2009.

The train will leave Vienna at 7:40 pm and reach Barcelona at 10:15 am the
next day, a journey of 14 hours 35 minutes, with stops in Zurich and Lyon.
Seats start at €49, a bed in a six-berth couchette at €89, and a private
cabin for two at €259.

"People want to travel without flying, but they don't want to lose a working
day to do it," said Clara Ostendorf, Nocturne Rail's chief executive. "You
board after dinner and you wake up by the sea."

The company estimates that a one-way trip produces about 18 kg of CO2 per
passenger, compared with 142 kg for the same journey by plane. A direct
flight takes 2 hours 25 minutes, but the company says that, door to door,
flying takes about five and a half hours.

Demand for night trains in Europe has grown quickly. According to an industry
survey published in January, passengers on overnight services rose from 1.1
million in 2021 to 1.9 million in 2022, 2.6 million in 2023, 3.2 million in
2024 and 3.7 million in 2025.

The route became possible after the rail infrastructure managers of Austria,
Switzerland, France and Spain agreed in June 2025 to share track slots for
overnight trains. Nocturne Rail ordered 24 sleeper carriages in September
2025, and the first eight were delivered in February.

Tickets go on sale on March 20. If the first season sells well, the company
plans to run the train every night from December.

Not everyone is convinced. Javier Molina, an independent transport economist,
said night trains "struggle to fill cabins outside the summer" and warned
that track works in southern France could cause delays in the first months.
