# A record loaf at the Fallowfield bake-off

This story is fictional. Fallowfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

The Fallowfield bake-off drew 320 visitors on Saturday, and the
winning loaf weighed 4.6 kg.

Entries grew over three years: 34 loaves in 2024, 41 in 2025, and 58
in 2026.

"I kneaded it for an hour. The secret is a slow rise," said Priya Raman, the winning baker.

The bake-off raises money for the town library. This year's event
raised $1,150.
