# How Fallowfield's flood warning works

This story is fictional. Fallowfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

When the Willow Brook gauge passes 4.0 m, the town's flood warning
runs in four stages. First, the river warden phones the council
office, and the office posts a river watch on the town noticeboard.
Second, the council issues a formal warning, and the riverside path
closes. Third, volunteers stack 200 sandbags at the community hall,
and the hall opens as a shelter. Fourth, if the gauge reaches 4.6 m,
the town opens 3 evacuation routes out of the riverside streets.

The system was last used in 2009, when the gauge reached 4.6 m and
12 homes took in water. The council reviews the stages every spring.
