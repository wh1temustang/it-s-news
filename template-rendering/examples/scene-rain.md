# Wrenfield's wettest day and a half

This story is fictional. Wrenfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

Wrenfield recorded 210 mm of rain in 36 hours, the most the town has
seen in a day and a half since its gauge started measuring in 1991.
The previous record was 160 mm, set in 2011.

The rain fell steadily from Tuesday morning to Wednesday evening, 36
hours without a break. By nightfall the Wren River had risen 3 metres,
but it stayed below the flood wall, and no homes were flooded.

"It is the wettest 36 hours in 35 years of records," said Ada Okafor,
the town's rain-gauge keeper. "The old record stood for 15 years."

The town council said it would review the flood wall in the spring.
