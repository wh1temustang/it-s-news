# Willow Creek's wettest day in 40 years

This story is fictional. Willow Creek is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

Willow Creek recorded 84 mm of rain on Tuesday, the most rain the town
has seen in a single day since its gauge started measuring in 1986.
The previous record was 61 mm, set in 1998.

The rain fell steadily from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., 16 hours without a break.
By nightfall the Willow River had risen 2 metres, but it stayed below
the flood wall, and no homes were flooded.

"It is the wettest day in 40 years of records," said Mara Ellison, the
town's rain-gauge keeper. "The old record stood for 28 years."

The town council said it would review the flood wall in the spring.
