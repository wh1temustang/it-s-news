# Fallowfield's deepest day of snow

This story is fictional. Fallowfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

Fallowfield recorded 42 cm of snow in 24 hours, the most in a single
day since records began in 1988. The previous record was 31 cm, set
in 2009.

The snow fell steadily from Monday morning to Tuesday morning, 24
hours without a break. By dawn the Willow Brook path was under 42 cm
of snow. The town's 3 ploughs cleared the main road by noon, and no
schools closed.

"It is the deepest single day of snow in 38 years of records," said
Mara Ellison, the town's snow measurer. "The old record stood for 17
years."

The town council said it would review the plough rota in the spring.
