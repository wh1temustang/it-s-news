# The river gauge passes the flood threshold

This story is fictional. Fallowfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

The Willow Brook gauge read 4.2 m at noon, above the flood threshold
of 4.0 m. The river had risen 0.3 m since the morning reading of
3.9 m, after a night of steady rain.

The previous high was 4.6 m, set in the flood of 2009. The council
said the riverside path would stay closed until the gauge falls back
below 4.0 m.

Mara Ellison, the town's river warden, said: "We watch the gauge, not
the sky."
