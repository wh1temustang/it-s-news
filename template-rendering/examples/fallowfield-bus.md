# Fallowfield tries a night bus

This story is fictional. Fallowfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

Fallowfield will run a night bus for 6 weeks from December 1, linking
the station, the hospital and the bakery quarter. The council approved
the trial on Tuesday by 7 votes to 2. A single fare costs $2, and buses
run every 30 minutes from 11 pm to 3 am.

"We have heard the night shift for years, and this trial is our
answer," said Councillor Priya Nair, chair of the transport committee.
"If the buses are full, the route stays."

Tom Okafor, a driver on the night rota, said the 3 am finish worries
him. "The last run ends at 3 am, and I am the one driving it home,"
he said.

June Park, who bakes through the night at the bakery quarter, would
take the bus four nights a week if it ran. The council will decide in
February whether the route becomes permanent.

Mara Chen, a nurse on the night shift at the hospital, said the bus
would get her home without a taxi. "The taxi home after a night shift
costs $18, and the bus costs $2," she said.
