# Council votes 7-2 to keep the pool open

This story is fictional. Fallowfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

Fallowfield council voted 7-2 on Thursday to keep the Riverside Pool
open through the winter, reversing a plan to close it from November
to March.

In March, the council proposed closing the pool for the winter to
save money. In June, residents handed in a petition with 1,240
signatures asking to keep it open. In September, the council costed
a trial winter opening at $48,000.

"The pool is where the town learns to swim. Closing it for four months every year was the wrong saving," said Councillor Tom Adeyemi.

The pool opens for the winter season on November 1.
