# A router that would not stop talking

This story is fictional. Wrenfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

A faulty router at Wrenfield Library began sending 600 connection
attempts a second to the router at the town hall on Thursday morning,
after a firmware update went wrong. The town hall router answered
every attempt, and its queue of 48 requests filled within a minute.

"It answered every knock, so the queue never drained," said Priya
Raman, the town's network keeper. "We pulled the plug on the library
router, and the town hall router cleared its queue in 20 seconds."

The library closed its computer room for the day while the router
was replaced. No town records were lost.
