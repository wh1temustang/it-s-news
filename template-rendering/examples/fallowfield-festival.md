# Fallowfield's summer festival, paid for three ways

This story is fictional. Fallowfield is an invented town, and every
number in it is made up for this example.

This year's summer festival costs $120,000, and the money comes from
three places. The town council granted $60,000, ticket sales brought
in $35,000, and local sponsors gave $25,000.

The council grant is the same as last year. Ticket sales rose from
$28,000 last year, and sponsors gave $5,000 more than last year.

The festival runs for 2 days and expects 4,000 visitors.
